...at least the 2x telephoto.

Went to a place called Point au Gaul near us. There is a fabulous sandy beach there.  My wife and I brought our student, one of our kids, and 2 of our dogs (the ones that listen when off leash).  

Things I discovered regarding the lens (and other things):
1. The quality of the image is great, because it uses and is designed for the main camera
2. The zoom doesn't zoom as nice as the 5x on the phone (obviously), but it gives you a little more oomph on the main camera, and the quality is much better.
3. It still gets some lens flare in the sun, which needs to be edited out. A CPL will do some correction there.
4. The bluetooth shutter has a lag sometimes, so not the best for action shots, unless firing burst mode, but then you lose RAW.  The first shot seems to be laggy, but that's probably a wake-up/handshake thing, otherwise, it is pretty instantaneous. I will need to get into the habit of half pressing/or pressing once before I want to shoot.
5. RAW is probably the problem too, because of file sizes....  So, switch off RAW, shoot your action, put RAW back when you have composed shots.  
6. Changing the lens attachment isn't bad for speed, but takes some fiddling with the bayonett. Shiftcam have put symbols on the lense to indicate top of the lens etc, like a real camera lens, to help you align things. Essentially lens swapping takes a little less than changing lenses on a Mirrorless or DSLR.
7. The weight wasn't bad. I was suprised by that, considering the massive size of the lens. Since the lens was new, and heavy, I was nervous re: the phone slipping out of the Progrip... I hope my grip tape shows up soon, and see if that experiment works. Most of the time, I held it vertically close to my body to prevent too much shake while moving.
