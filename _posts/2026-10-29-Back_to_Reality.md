Well, after about 36 hours of travel, we are back from Greece and back in our quiet existence in Grand Bank.  The planned setup with the camera worked pretty good. I mainly used the 2x telephoto lens, and for a FEW shots had the CPL on, and I only used the tripod once for a few star shots. Didn't even touch the long range macro.  I also didn't trust my waterproof case, so I used a waterproof bag thing, and it was garbage for underwater shots, but it DID keep my phone dry.  While shooting RAW, all day, I usually had enough juice in the phone, plus the battery in the ProGrip. 

Santorini? OMG, so photogenic.
