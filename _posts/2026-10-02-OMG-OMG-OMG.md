Ok, so I ordered some new lenses for my iPhone from [Shiftcam](https://www.shiftcam.com/). I have been heavily into their eco-system for awhile, and have many of their products. Really good quality optics, and the build quality is simply ridonkulous.  Their packaging is really super classy too.  I didn't take an unboxing video, because I was too excited (oops).

I acquired 2 lenses this time (tho I have other, older generation lenses). The [2x telephoto](https://www.shiftcam.com/products/v-series-60mm-tele-lens) and the [Long Range Macro](https://www.shiftcam.com/products/v-series-75mm-macro-lens) because if you know me, you know I love my macro shots.  

They are HUGE, but solid, and come with great lens caps (both front and back), and a zippered pouch for them. Really well thought out too, as they fit well with their ProGrip (which I have too).  I won't need the pouches too much, as I have (surprise, surprise) a [sling bag](https://www.shiftcam.com/products/wrkflw-sling) also designed by Shiftcam which will hold the lenses safely.
