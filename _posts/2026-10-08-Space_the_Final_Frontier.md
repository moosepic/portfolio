It was a clear night, though cold, so I layered up and put on my toque, and went down to our breakwater to get away from light pollution. I got my phone, my ProGrip, and attached the V-Series 2x, and tried a few night sky shots.

Well, the grip gave me enough stability that I was able to pull off 10s Night Mode shots. If I had a tripod, I could've probably managed the max of 30s.

Long story short? Genuinely impressed.  Mainly at the grip giving me enough stability to take the 10s shots. I'm used to 3s max.
