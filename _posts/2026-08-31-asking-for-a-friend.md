So, just wondering, why aren't there any external flash options for an iPhone, that aren't thousands of dollars? 

There was a small one, called an 'iBlazr2' by this small Ukranian company called 'Concepter', which was a nice little bluetooth enabled one. This was back in 2016.  Slight problem was that it required a their 3rd party camera app called 'Shotlight' which is no longer available. The company still exists, but the product is no longer supported. That said, the product still WORKS, but can no longer be used as a remote flash. It can be a continuous light, and can still (I think) work as a bluetooth shutter trigger. Their old Kickstart campaign is still searchable online.

There is a company called Profoto, that makes them, but these are top of the line professional ones, that are going to cost at least $1000US, and require using their 3rd party camera app as well. 

Anyone know of any? I don't use a flash all that often, so Profoto is out of reach, cost-wise. I'm not a huge fan of constant lights (I have one of those already)
